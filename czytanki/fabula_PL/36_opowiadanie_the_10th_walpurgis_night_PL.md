#### Bus’s Backdoor

His vengeful anguish drifted farther and farther away until even that sound scattered without a trace. By the time my vision settled, all that remained was the familiar sight of our bus's corridor and the long row of doors lining it.
**Faust:** ... It seems that this is where this scenario ends.
**Dante:** That felt a lot longer than usual...
**Faust:** Yes. Which will make calculating its variables considerably more complex.
And whenever the scenario seemed poised to close, it stitched another fragment onto its edge as though it refused to let the thread end there...
**Dante:** Faust, there's something I've been wondering about...
**Faust:** ... Yes?
If showing us someone Effloresce an E.G.O had been the sole point of this scenario, why make us trail Philip's footsteps so closely? If it were, all it had to do was show us the final flicker of the process.
Instead, it felt as though the scenario wanted us to walk beside Philip's story itself.
And if it had needed to be built that way...
**Dante:** Do you think the scenario was stitched together this strangely because it wanted us to see the *entire* process of Efflorescence?
**Faust:** ... That is possible.
**Dante:** .......
**Yi Sang:** Perhaps... the course of a Reception itself bears a significance no lesser than obtaining the books to fill the shelves.
... I considered asking Yi Sang why that would be the case, but it didn't feel like there was much point in asking.
Something told me that, if the scenario had chosen to speak vaguely, then it probably had a good reason for doing so.
For now, all we could do was walk it again and look for whatever might have slipped past us the first time.
**Dante:** All right, then. Let's give it another go and make sure that we haven't missed anything.
