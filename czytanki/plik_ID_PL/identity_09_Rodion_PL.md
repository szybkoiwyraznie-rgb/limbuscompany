# Limbus Company — Rodion: Identity

*Profil postaci: dane liczbowe z tabel gry, opisy z plików lokalizacji.*

## Zakres i zasady odczytu

- Teksty w wersji `pl`; wpisy bez przekładu pozostają w oryginale (angielskim); liczby z `StaticData` (wartości bazowe, bez E.G.O i bonusów).
- **Ulepszenie** = Uptie (gaksung) 1–4; tabela umiejętności pokazuje tylko wiersze, w których coś się zmienia. Moc ataku = moc bazowa + moc monety za każdą wyrzuconą reszkę/orła wg reguł gry.
- **Poziom ataku/obrony** to korekta względem poziomu postaci.
- **Odporności**: mnożnik obrażeń danego typu (×2 = Fatal, ×0,5 = Endured).
- Elementy bez opisu w danych są oznaczone „—”, nie zgadywane.

## Akta więźnia

> Grzesznica, po której jaskrawo widać przykre piętno urodzenia w Zaułkach.
> Lubi pieniądze, hazard i zakłady, dlatego wymaga baczniejszej uwagi Kierownika.
> Na polecenia ma zwyczaj odpowiadać nieudolnym mrugnięciem albo głupim żartem,
> dlatego czasem zalecamy okazać jej surowość przełożonego.
> Nieregularnie popada w przygnębienie; Kierownik nie może wtedy nic
> zrobić, najskuteczniej jest spokojnie przeczekać.

- **Identity Acquisition:** I distinctly remember you people calling my work "unrefined," "savage"... So why are ya having trouble looking away now? Look at you—it's like you're in love.

Heard something interesting when I visited District 23's gallery last time. Apparently, ceratotaurus tongues are great for sandwich fillings. Thought I'd try making one for myself!
- **Evening Greeting:** Ever heard a wolf howl in the middle of the night? That low, heavy hum, brushing against every nerve fiber like fog creeping under your skin... *chuckle* The moment I heard it, I knew. That was it—I knew exactly the perfect beast I wanted to reshape and paint over.
- **Chatter #1:** Ta-da! Whaddaya think? "Artwork: Dead Lake". With the Great Lake's patchwork texture as my inspiration, I took the hides of different Whales from the lake and stitched them together. Call it my signature piece! Hehe... It might even be good enough for a VIP auction, who knows? Or maybe even a pedestal of its own at the Biennale...
- **Chatter #2:** The moment I saw that monster of a bear, I realized this arm had to be mine. Check it out: the springy tension in the muscle fibers, the coarse, stiff fur, and these unbreakable claws! I thought it'd be a bold, creative attempt to stitch and mesh this part with another creature that had a slightly different twist. The result? Jackpot, of course!
- **Chatter #3:** Hmm... yeah, no. Turn this in as is, and the best I can give you is a D+. Art's supposed to be ferocious, vicious—enough to seize attention with just a single glimpse. Still too basic to do that? Then at least try something that drags the spectators in by the collar—give them an impression of a lifetime. That's what Fauvism's all about, you know this! If you'd rather create some half-hearted nonsense like this, go join another school.

If you've already breathed in the sight, then let it dig a scar in your sensibilities for life. That raw, visceral affect—that's the only fauve touch laypeople like you can add to art, isn't it?

There—just a few tendons tugged, and it all clicks right into place, clean and snug... Hey, wait up. Are ya not even gonna pretend to hear me out before making a break for it? Well, if you don't wanna... Guess I'll just have to carve it into your bones instead. That tends to leave an unforgettable *impression*.
