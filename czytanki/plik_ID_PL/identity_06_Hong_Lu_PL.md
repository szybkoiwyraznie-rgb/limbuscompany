# Limbus Company — Hong Lu: Identity

*Profil postaci: dane liczbowe z tabel gry, opisy z plików lokalizacji.*

## Zakres i zasady odczytu

- Teksty w wersji `pl`; wpisy bez przekładu pozostają w oryginale (angielskim); liczby z `StaticData` (wartości bazowe, bez E.G.O i bonusów).
- **Ulepszenie** = Uptie (gaksung) 1–4; tabela umiejętności pokazuje tylko wiersze, w których coś się zmienia. Moc ataku = moc bazowa + moc monety za każdą wyrzuconą reszkę/orła wg reguł gry.
- **Poziom ataku/obrony** to korekta względem poziomu postaci.
- **Odporności**: mnożnik obrażeń danego typu (×2 = Fatal, ×0,5 = Endured).
- Elementy bez opisu w danych są oznaczone „—”, nie zgadywane.

## Akta więźnia

> A Sinner who had lived an affluent life before becoming our company's asset.
> As such, he has a certain admiration for a free life;
> but occasionally, he may make ignorant and unsavory questions in regards
> to the food cooked by a certain other Sinner or other aspects of the low-lives' lowly culture.
> This can cause significant irritation for the other Sinners.
> However, it is important to note that no sarcastic undertones are contained in his curiosity-driven inquiries,
> so it is ill-advised to let them get physical over it.

- **Identity Acquisition:** Let us explore together—the pulsations of your remaining life.
- **Morning Greeting:** Those who lead an artist's life ought to cultivate the habit of rising early. Generous morning hours provide ample time for exhibition preparation and last-minute touch-ups.
- **Afternoon Greeting:** I'm sorry, Faust—we'll have to postpone our scheduled critique from today to tomorrow. A fellow Maestro has sought my counsel on gallery management, so I will be visiting theirs for a spell. I entrust my gallery to your hands in my absence. This shouldn't need mentioning, but please make sure not to let Rodion, that drunkard, take a single step into my gallery.
- **Evening Greeting:** In the small hours of the night, and the subsequent hours in anticipation of dawn's incipience... the incessant whispers of inspiration find me like divine revelation. For us artists, that is the proverbial "golden hour." The aorta pulses like the rivers that flow in the City's elsewheres, while the capillaries' stems and extremities spread like a sheet of star-studded firmament. Oh... How fascinating, that such macroscopic gaze can be drawn from such minusculus! Yet, once the enchanted hours pass... I am confronted by the realization that my creation is middling at best.
- **Chatter #1:** I venture to say my corner of the Corridors is hardly ever graced by visitors. Still, with such a large audience peering through my jade eye as my witness, I must remain vigilant in keeping my exhibits well-cared for.
- **Chatter #2:** "Each pulsation of the venous blue, currents to where the bloodroot red withdrew." A line from a poem of mine, visualizing the contrast between veins and arteries. I don't enjoy it as much as I do sculpture of the corpus, but I do find composing poetry an enjoyable pastime. I often write a short verse when I come across a piece of art I enjoy.
- **Chatter #3:** I had once tried, despite my insufficient knowledge, to "pick my brain." It was a failed attempt to adjust the volume of my brain by carving and trimming it. Had I known the sheer difficulty of molding one's own brain when limited to relying only on the tactile senses of the hands... Well, I wouldn't have had to cover a part of my corpus with this headpiece.
